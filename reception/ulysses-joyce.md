## The transformation

The Homeric correspondences are structural provocations rather than a one-to-one retelling. Leopold Bloom, Stephen Dedalus, and Molly Bloom refract Odysseus, Telemachus, and Penelope while styles change radically from episode to episode.[^adapt]

- Which character receives interiority or authority?
- Is homecoming restored, refused, or made impossible?
