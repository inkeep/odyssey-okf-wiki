## Confidence ladder

| Level | Meaning in this wiki |
| --- | --- |
| Structurally checked | Frontmatter parses; Markdown lint and internal links pass |
| Source-linked | The page identifies local evidence and cites it at the relevant claim |
| Primary-text checked | A claim can be checked against the preserved Greek text or complete translation |
| Interpretively argued | The page distinguishes textual observation from analytical inference |
| Expert reviewed | A qualified human classicist has reviewed the claim; this level is not claimed globally |
