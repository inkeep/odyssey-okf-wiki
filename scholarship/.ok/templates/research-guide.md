> [!NOTE]
> What question this page lets a reader answer for themselves.

Pages in this folder set their own headings — a bibliography, a verification record and a counting method need different shapes, and no two share one. Two obligations are not optional.

State the method precisely enough that someone holding the preserved sources can repeat it and get the same result.[^butler]

Then give it a section on where it is imperfect: the cases it gets wrong or cannot decide, and any occasion it was found wrong and corrected. A method claim that hides its edges is worse than no claim at all.

Check every factual assertion about the source files before writing it — counts, line positions, what a file does and does not contain. In this repository, unverified claims on method pages have been the single most common defect, and each one was falsifiable with one command.
