## Overview

The oikos joins family, property, labor, ritual, reputation, and political authority.[^butler]

## In the poem

Suitors consume livestock and court Penelope; servants choose or endure allegiances; the bow and bed materialize dynastic continuity.[^butler]

## Why it matters

Odysseus's sovereignty is domestic before it is civic, and Book 24 shows that household victory still needs public peace.
