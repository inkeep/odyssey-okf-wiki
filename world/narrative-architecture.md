## Overview

The plot begins near the end of Odysseus's absence, separates father and son, then lets the hero narrate earlier adventures retrospectively.[^britannica]

## In the poem

Books 1–4 form the Telemachy; 5–8 bring Odysseus to the Phaeacian court; 9–12 contain his first-person wanderings; 13–24 stage return and recognition.[^butler]

## Why it matters

Delayed identification turns the story of travel into a story about who controls knowledge.
