## Overview

The household depends on enslaved and dependent labor: herding, nursing, weaving, provisioning, and information work.[^butler]

## In the poem

Eumaeus, Eurycleia, Philoetius, Melanthius, Melantho, and others are judged through loyalty, but their choices occur under unequal power.[^butler]

## Why it matters

Modern reading must hold together the poem's moral distinctions and the coercive institution that structures them.
