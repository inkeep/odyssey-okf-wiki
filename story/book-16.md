## Why this book matters

Recognition is emotional but immediately strategic: kinship becomes conspiracy.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telemachus reaches the hut where a beggar is sitting by the fire | He sends Eumaeus into town with news for his mother | Father and son are alone together for the first time in twenty years |
| A claim of identity here would be both incredible and dangerous | Athena restores Odysseus's form and he declares himself | Telemachus takes him for a god until he insists; then they weep together |
| Two men cannot fight a hundred and eight | They plan: hide the arms, tell no one, absorb every insult without answering | Kinship becomes conspiracy — while the suitors, their ambush failed, debate murder openly |
