## Why this book matters

Hospitality is a process of careful recognition, not instant trust.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He must cross a strange city whose people are cold to outsiders | Athena wraps him in mist and guides him in the shape of a young girl | He reaches the hall unchallenged |
| A stranger in the hall has no claim until someone grants him one | He clasps Arete's knees before he speaks, then sits down in the ashes | Echeneus shames the court into seating him, and passage is promised |
| Arete recognizes the clothes he wears as her own household's work | She questions him; he tells of Calypso and the wreck but withholds his name | Hospitality proceeds on partial disclosure — Alcinous offers both his daughter and the ship |
