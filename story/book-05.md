## Why this book matters

The first sight of Odysseus defines him through grief, craft, endurance, and an uncompromising preference for mortal belonging.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seven years of detention on Ogygia, with the gods' decision still unenforced | Athena raises the case again and Zeus sends Hermes with an order | Calypso yields, protesting the standard the gods apply to goddesses |
| Calypso offers immortality on condition that he stay | Odysseus refuses without insulting her, praising his mortal wife | He chooses a mortal home over deathlessness and builds his raft |
| Poseidon sees him at sea and smashes the raft | He clings to the wreck, takes Ino's veil only when it breaks up, and swims two nights and two days | He lands battered on Scheria and sleeps buried under leaves |
