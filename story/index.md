## Story Section

* [Book 10: Winds, giants, and Circe](./book-10.md) - Aeolus's wind bag is opened by suspicious crewmen. The Laestrygonians destroy every ship but Odysseus's own. Circe transforms scouts until Hermes equips him to resist.
* [Book 11: The dead and the future](./book-11.md) - Odysseus sacrifices at the edge of Hades. Elpenor asks burial; Tiresias prophesies; Anticlea reports home; Agamemnon and Achilles redefine return and glory.
* [Book 12: Sirens, Scylla, Charybdis, and the cattle](./book-12.md) - Circe's instructions guide the ship past the Sirens and between Scylla and Charybdis. On Thrinacia, Eurylochus persuades the starving crew to kill Helios's cattle; Zeus destroys the ship.
* [Book 13: Departure from Scheria](./book-13.md) - The Phaeacians deliver the sleeping Odysseus and his gifts to Ithaca. Poseidon petrifies their ship. Athena reveals the island and plans disguise.
* [Book 14: The swineherd](./book-14.md) - Disguised as a beggar, Odysseus is welcomed by Eumaeus and invents a Cretan biography in which he is sold into slavery. The swineherd's loyalty is tested without either man naming it.
* [Book 15: Telemachus returns](./book-15.md) - Athena recalls Telemachus from Sparta. He avoids the suitors' ambush, entrusts the seer Theoclymenus to Piraeus, and goes first to the hut of Eumaeus.
* [Book 16: Father and son](./book-16.md) - Athena restores Odysseus's appearance for Telemachus. After disbelief, father and son recognize each other and plan the suitors' destruction.
* [Book 17: The beggar enters the palace](./book-17.md) - Telemachus returns to Penelope. Eumaeus leads Odysseus to town; Melanthius abuses him, Argos recognizes him, and Antinous strikes him in the hall.
* [Book 18: The beggars and the household women](./book-18.md) - Odysseus defeats Irus, warns Amphinomus, endures Melantho's insults, and hears Penelope draw gifts from the suitors.
* [Book 19: The scar and the interview](./book-19.md) - Penelope questions the stranger, who offers a detailed false account and predicts Odysseus's return. Eurycleia washes his feet and recognizes the scar.
* [Book 1: Athena and Telemachus](./book-01.md) - The gods debate Odysseus's detention. Athena, disguised as Mentes, urges Telemachus to confront the suitors and seek news.
* [Book 20: Omens before the contest](./book-20.md) - Odysseus receives encouraging signs. Telemachus asserts authority; Ctesippus throws a heifer's foot; Theoclymenus sees the hall darkened by death.
* [Book 21: The bow](./book-21.md) - Penelope announces the archery contest. Telemachus nearly strings the bow; the suitors fail. Odysseus reveals himself to Eumaeus and Philoetius, strings the bow, and shoots through the axes.
* [Book 22: The slaughter of the suitors](./book-22.md) - Odysseus kills Antinous and rejects Eurymachus's settlement. With Telemachus and loyal servants he defeats the suitors; Athena intervenes in Mentor's form.
* [Book 23: Penelope's test](./book-23.md) - Eurycleia announces the return. Penelope remains cautious until her reference to moving the marriage bed provokes Odysseus to reveal its secret construction.
* [Book 24: Laertes and peace](./book-24.md) - The suitors' shades reach Hades. Odysseus tests and recognizes Laertes. Relatives of the dead attack, Laertes kills Eupithes, and Athena imposes peace.
* [Book 2: The Ithacan assembly](./book-02.md) - Telemachus calls Ithaca's first assembly in years. The suitors reject blame, Halitherses reads an eagle omen, and Telemachus sails for Pylos.
* [Book 3: Pylos and the memory of Troy](./book-03.md) - At Pylos, Nestor receives Telemachus during a sacrifice and recounts the divided returns of the Greeks. Pisistratus escorts him onward.
* [Book 4: Sparta, Helen, and Menelaus](./book-04.md) - Menelaus and Helen host Telemachus. Stories of Odysseus at Troy and Proteus's report confirm he lives on Calypso's island.
* [Book 5: Calypso and the open sea](./book-05.md) - Hermes orders Calypso to release Odysseus. He builds a raft, rejects immortality without home, survives Poseidon's storm, and reaches Scheria.
* [Book 6: Nausicaa at the river](./book-06.md) - Athena sends Nausicaa to wash clothes. She meets the naked castaway, responds to his tactful supplication, and directs him to the city.
* [Book 7: The palace of the Phaeacians](./book-07.md) - Veiled by Athena, Odysseus enters Alcinous's palace and supplicates Arete. The rulers promise conveyance but probe his identity.
* [Book 8: Songs, games, and tears](./book-08.md) - Demodocus sings of Troy; Odysseus weeps. Athletic tension gives way to gifts, and another song forces Alcinous to ask the stranger's name.
* [Book 9: The Cyclops](./book-09.md) - Odysseus names himself and begins his retrospective tale: Cicons, Lotus-Eaters, then Polyphemus. The Noman trick saves survivors, but the boast summons Poseidon's curse.
