## Why this book matters

The returning king reads his kingdom from below, while an animal achieves the recognition withheld by humans.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telemachus returns to a mother who wants proof and a hall that wanted him dead | He reports what Nestor and Menelaus told him, and Theoclymenus prophesies outright | Penelope hears that Odysseus is alive and near, and still withholds belief |
| The road into town exposes the beggar to whoever holds power along it | Melanthius kicks and abuses him; Odysseus absorbs it without retaliating | Self-command is established as the discipline the whole return depends on |
| He crosses his own threshold unrecognized by everyone in it | Argos, lying neglected on the dung heap, lifts his head and knows him | The dog dies having given the recognition no person offers — and Antinous answers the beggar's request with a thrown footstool |
