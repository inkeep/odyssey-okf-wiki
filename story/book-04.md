## Why this book matters

Nested stories establish Odysseus before he appears and make truth depend on narrators whose motives remain complex.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telemachus reaches a wedding feast at Sparta and cannot bring himself to ask | Menelaus praises the absent Odysseus until the boy weeps into his cloak | Helen names the visitor from his likeness to his father |
| Memory of Troy is unbearable and the two hosts' accounts do not agree | Helen drugs the wine, and each tells a story of Odysseus at Troy | Truth about the hero becomes a matter of who is narrating |
| Menelaus was once becalmed on Pharos with no way home | He ambushes Proteus and holds the shifting god until he answers | Proteus reports Odysseus alive on Calypso's island — while at home the suitors lay an ambush |
