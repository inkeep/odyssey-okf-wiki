## Why this book matters

A functioning ritual community teaches by contrast: memory becomes counsel rather than paralysis.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telemachus arrives among strangers at a sacrifice with no news and no nerve | Athena as Mentor pushes him to question the old king directly | He is received as a guest and asks after his father |
| What became of the Achaeans after Troy is scattered and half-remembered | Nestor recounts the quarrel at Troy and the divided homecomings | Return is revealed as plural and contingent — many homecomings, not one |
| Agamemnon's murder hangs over every returning king | Nestor holds up Orestes' vengeance as the pattern for a son | Telemachus is given a model of filial duty and sent on to Sparta |
