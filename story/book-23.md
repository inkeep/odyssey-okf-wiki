## Why this book matters

The final marital recognition belongs to shared knowledge: identity is proved through a collaborative history built into the house.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eurycleia runs upstairs with the news that the master has come home | Penelope refuses it — a god, she says, may have killed them | Her caution outlasts the slaughter itself |
| Husband and wife sit facing each other across the fire, not speaking | Telemachus rebukes her hardness; she answers that they have signs known only to the two of them | The test is hers to set, and she sets it in private |
| She tells the nurse to move the bed out of the chamber for him | Odysseus breaks — the bed is rooted in a living olive trunk he cut and built the room around | Shared secret knowledge does what the scar and the bow could not, and the night is given back to them |
