## Why this book matters

Public speech does not restore order, but speaking creates Telemachus as a political subject.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twenty years without an assembly leave the suitors' plunder unnamed in public | Telemachus convenes Ithaca and accuses them before the people | The wrong is finally stated in public, but the assembly will not act on it |
| Antinous shifts the blame to Penelope and exposes the trick of the shroud | Telemachus refuses to drive his mother from the house | Deadlock: the suitors disclaim fault and the feasting continues |
| Zeus sends two eagles, and Halitherses reads them as slaughter | Eurymachus mocks the omen and threatens the seer | Prophecy is publicly discredited, so Athena readies a ship in secret — taking Telemachus's own shape to gather a crew, having already appeared to him as Mentor and going on to sail with him in that form |
