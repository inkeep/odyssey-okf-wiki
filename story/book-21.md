## Why this book matters

An heirloom of guest-friendship becomes the proof of embodied identity and the mechanism of restored control.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Penelope ends the waiting by setting a test she believes no one can pass | She brings out the bow Iphitus gave Odysseus and announces the contest of the axes | An heirloom of guest-friendship becomes the instrument of judgment |
| Telemachus and the suitors take their turns and fail | Eurymachus reads the failure as public shame; Antinous defers it to a feast day | Their inadequacy is demonstrated in front of witnesses rather than merely asserted |
| He cannot take the hall without the loyal herdsmen and barred doors | He shows the scar to Eumaeus and Philoetius and gives them their orders | He strings the bow as a bard fits a string, shoots clean through the axes, and nods Telemachus to arms |
