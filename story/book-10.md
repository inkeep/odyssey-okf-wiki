## Why this book matters

Each harbor tests leadership under uncertainty; the surviving group shrinks as trust and reconnaissance fail.[^butler]

## Dramatic movement

| Pressure | Response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ithaca is in sight after Aeolus's gift of the bagged winds | Odysseus keeps the bag's contents secret and finally sleeps at the helm | The crew opens it expecting gold; the storm drives them back, and Aeolus turns them away |
| The harbor at Telepylus looks sheltered and safe | Every ship but Odysseus's own moors inside it | The Laestrygonians spear the crews, and every ship inside the harbour is lost — eleven of the twelve, since Butler counts the fleet at "twelve ships" in Book 9 and has Odysseus moor his own outside |
| Circe turns the scouting party into swine | Hermes gives him the herb moly and the counter-instruction | He resists the drug, binds her by oath, and frees his men — then stays a year until the crew must remind him of home |
