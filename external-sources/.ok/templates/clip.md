## Source record

Set `type: source`, `source_url`, `date_fetched`, `preservation`, `author`, and `rights` in the new document's frontmatter. Record factual bibliographic metadata and the source's coverage in original words.

- Default to metadata-only for copyrighted web pages.
- Preserve full text or binary assets only when redistribution is permitted, and record byte count and SHA-256 when you do.
- Keep third-party prose, images, reviews, and promotional copy out unless clearly licensed — including phrasing borrowed from publisher copy.

## Use in this wiki

Explain which local claims this record supports, and distinguish what the record itself carries from what merely exists on the page it points at. A page citing this record must not assert more than the record holds.

## Rights

The applicable licence, the rightsholder, any modifications, and jurisdiction caveats. Say plainly what is **not** reproduced here.
