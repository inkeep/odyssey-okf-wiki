## Captured source record

Britannica identifies the *Odyssey* as an epic poem in twenty-four books traditionally attributed to Homer. Its central action is Odysseus's return to Ithaca after the Trojan War, while Penelope and Telemachus contend with suitors occupying the household. The reference overview distinguishes the Telemachy, Odysseus's wanderings, and the Ithacan return-and-recognition plot.
