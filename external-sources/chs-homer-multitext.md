## Source record

The Center for Hellenic Studies project page identifies the Homer Multitext as a digital scholarly project concerned with the transmission, variation, and performance traditions of Homeric poetry. The page credits Casey Dué and Mary Ebbott as editors and Christopher Blackwell and Neel Smith as information architects.
