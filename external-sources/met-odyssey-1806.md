## Metropolitan Museum of Art — *The Odyssey* (1806–13)

> [!CAUTION]
> Metadata-only record: the catalog page was consulted, but its raw response was not preserved locally. Use only the cataloged facts below.

## Captured catalog facts

The Metropolitan Museum of Art catalogs an 1806–13 edition of *The Odyssey* translated by Alexander Pope. Its credited artists include Edward Francis Burney, Henry Howard, Henry Singleton, Henry Fuseli, Richard Westall, and Robert Smirke, with engravers including James Stow, James Fittler, and Joseph Collyer.
