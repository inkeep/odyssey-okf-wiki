## Source record

Kristen Lopez’s article for History surveys modern afterlives of *The Odyssey* across literature, film, television, audio, and comics. Works discussed include Tennyson’s “Ulysses,” Joyce’s *Ulysses*, *Le Mépris*, *The Simpsons*, *Odyssey: The Podcast*, Margaret Atwood’s *The Penelopiad*, and *ODY-C*.

The article also has a dedicated section on *O Brother, Where Art Thou?* (2000), crediting Joel and Ethan Coen, which an earlier version of this record omitted from its list.
