> Odysseus's kinsman and prominent subordinate.

## Role in the poem

He warns against Circe, resists returning to her house, and later persuades the starving crew to kill Helios's cattle.[^butler]

## Interpretive profile

He is both necessary dissenter and disastrous counter-leader, exposing the limits of command.[^butler]
