# Index

## Character

* [Achilles](./achilles.md) - Greatest Greek warrior at Troy, encountered among the dead.
* [Actoris](./actoris.md) - Servant associated with Penelope's marriage
* [Aegisthus](./aegisthus.md) - Usurper who seduces Clytemnestra and, with her, kills Agamemnon.
* [Aegyptius](./aegyptius.md) - Elder who opens Ithaca's first assembly in twenty years
* [Aeolus](./aeolus.md) - Keeper of the winds and ruler of a floating island.
* [Agamemnon](./agamemnon.md) - Commander at Troy whose shade recounts his murder after returning home.
* [Agelaus](./agelaus.md) - Prominent suitor in the final battle
* [Ajax](./ajax.md) - Greek warrior whose quarrel with Odysseus continued beyond death.
* [Alcinous](./alcinous.md) - King of the Phaeacians and father of Nausicaa.
* [Alcmene](./alcmene.md) - Mother of Heracles
* [Amphimedon](./amphimedon.md) - Suitor whose shade recounts the slaughter
* [Amphinomus](./amphinomus.md) - Relatively restrained suitor from Dulichium.
* [Amphithea](./amphithea.md) - Maternal grandmother of Odysseus
* [Anticlea](./anticlea.md) - Mother of Odysseus, encountered among the dead.
* [Antilochus](./antilochus.md) - Son of Nestor remembered among the honored dead
* [Antinous](./antinous.md) - Most aggressive leader among Penelope's suitors.
* [Antiope](./antiope.md) - Mother of Amphion and Zethus
* [Aphrodite](./aphrodite.md) - Goddess whose affair with Ares is sung by Demodocus
* [Apollo](./apollo.md) - God invoked in song, prophecy, plague, and archery
* [Ares](./ares.md) - War god and Aphrodite's lover in Demodocus's song
* [Arete](./arete.md) - Queen of the Phaeacians, wife of Alcinous, and influential judge within the palace.
* [Argos](./argos.md) - Odysseus's aged hunting dog.
* [Ariadne](./ariadne.md) - Daughter of Minos remembered in Hades
* [Artemis](./artemis.md) - Goddess repeatedly imagined as an agent of sudden death
* [Athena](./athena.md) - Goddess of strategic intelligence and Odysseus's chief divine ally.
* [Autolycus](./autolycus.md) - Maternal grandfather of Odysseus and master of cunning
* [Calypso](./calypso.md) - Immortal nymph who keeps Odysseus on Ogygia.
* [Charybdis](./charybdis.md) - Devouring whirlpool opposite Scylla.
* [Chloris](./chloris.md) - Queen of Neleus and mother in the Pylian line
* [Circe](./circe.md) - Goddess and enchantress on Aeaea.
* [Clitoneus](./clitoneus.md) - Phaeacian prince and runner
* [Clytemnestra](./clytemnestra.md) - Wife who, with Aegisthus, brings about Agamemnon's death.
* [Ctesippus](./ctesippus.md) - Wealthy suitor notorious for violent mockery.
* [Ctimene](./ctimene.md) - Sister of Odysseus
* [Demodocus](./demodocus.md) - Blind bard at the Phaeacian court.
* [Demoptolemus](./demoptolemus.md) - Suitor killed by Odysseus
* [Dolius](./dolius.md) - Aged servant associated with Laertes's farm.
* [Echeneus](./echeneus.md) - Elder Phaeacian counselor
* [Eidothea](./eidothea.md) - Daughter of Proteus who aids Menelaus.
* [Elatus](./elatus.md) - Suitor killed by Eumaeus
* [Elpenor](./elpenor.md) - Young crewman who dies unnoticed on Circe's roof.
* [Eos](./eos.md) - Dawn goddess whose recurring arrival structures epic time
* [Epicaste](./epicaste.md) - Mother and wife of Oedipus, also known as Jocasta
* [Eriphyle](./eriphyle.md) - Woman condemned in heroic memory for betraying her husband
* [Eumaeus](./eumaeus.md) - Loyal swineherd of Odysseus.
* [Eupithes](./eupithes.md) - Father of Antinous and leader of the suitors' relatives.
* [Euryalus](./euryalus.md) - Phaeacian athlete who insults and later compensates Odysseus
* [Eurybates](./eurybates.md) - Odysseus's herald and trusted follower
* [Eurycleia](./eurycleia.md) - Old nurse of Odysseus and Telemachus.
* [Eurydamas](./eurydamas.md) - Suitor and failed interpreter of Penelope's intentions
* [Eurylochus](./eurylochus.md) - Odysseus's kinsman and prominent subordinate.
* [Eurymachus](./eurymachus.md) - Smooth-speaking suitor and rival leader to Antinous.
* [Eurymedusa](./eurymedusa.md) - Attendant of Nausicaa
* [Eurynomus](./eurynomus.md) - Suitor named among Penelope's unwanted court
* [Hades](./hades.md) - Lord of the realm of the dead
* [Halitherses](./halitherses.md) - Ithacan seer and elder.
* [Halius](./halius.md) - Phaeacian prince and dancer
* [Helen](./helen.md) - Queen of Sparta whose elopement precipitated the Trojan War.
* [Helios](./helios.md) - Sun god whose sacred cattle graze on Thrinacia.
* [Hephaestus](./hephaestus.md) - Divine smith who traps Aphrodite and Ares
* [Heracles](./heracles.md) - Earlier hero glimpsed in the underworld.
* [Hermes](./hermes.md) - Divine messenger and helper of Odysseus.
* [Ino](./ino.md) - Sea goddess who aids the shipwrecked Odysseus.
* [Iphimedeia](./iphimedeia.md) - Mother of Otus and Ephialtes
* [Iphitus](./iphitus.md) - Friend who once gave Odysseus the bow used in the contest.
* [Irus](./irus.md) - Ithacan beggar used by the suitors for errands and sport.
* [Laertes](./laertes.md) - Aged father of Odysseus, living withdrawn from the palace.
* [Lampetie](./lampetie.md) - Daughter of Helios who guards his cattle
* [Laodamas](./laodamas.md) - Son of Alcinous and accomplished Phaeacian athlete
* [Leda](./leda.md) - Mother of Castor and Polydeuces
* [Leocritus](./leocritus.md) - Suitor who dismisses the assembly and threatens resistance
* [Leodes](./leodes.md) - Suitors' diviner and first competitor in the bow contest.
* [Maron](./maron.md) - Priest of Apollo who gives Odysseus potent wine
* [Medon](./medon.md) - Herald in Odysseus's household.
* [Megara](./megara.md) - Daughter of Creon and wife of Heracles
* [Melanthius](./melanthius.md) - Goatherd who has sided with the suitors.
* [Melantho](./melantho.md) - Maidservant aligned with the suitors and lover of Eurymachus.
* [Menelaus](./menelaus.md) - King of Sparta, husband of Helen, and veteran of Troy.
* [Mentes](./mentes.md) - Taphian leader whose identity Athena adopts
* [Mentor](./mentor.md) - Ithacan elder whose appearance Athena often adopts.
* [Minos](./minos.md) - Judge among the dead
* [Nausicaa](./nausicaa.md) - Phaeacian princess who encounters the shipwrecked Odysseus.
* [Nausithous](./nausithous.md) - Former king and founder of Phaeacian Scheria
* [Nestor](./nestor.md) - Aged king of Pylos and veteran of Troy.
* [Noemon](./noemon.md) - Ithacan who lends Telemachus a ship
* [Odysseus](./odysseus.md) - King of Ithaca, veteran of Troy, master of disguise and difficult homecomer.
* [Orestes](./orestes.md) - Son of Agamemnon and avenger of his father's murder.
* [Orion](./orion.md) - Great hunter glimpsed in Hades
* [Patroclus](./patroclus.md) - Companion of Achilles seen among the dead
* [Penelope](./penelope.md) - Queen of Ithaca, Odysseus's wife, and strategist of delay.
* [Perimedes](./perimedes.md) - Companion who assists Odysseus in ritual and travel
* [Persephone](./persephone.md) - Queen of the dead beside Hades
* [Phaethusa](./phaethusa.md) - Daughter of Helios and guardian of his flocks
* [Phemius](./phemius.md) - Bard compelled to perform for the suitors.
* [Philoetius](./philoetius.md) - Loyal cowherd of Odysseus.
* [Pisander the suitor](./pisander.md) - Suitor who gives Penelope a necklace
* [Pisistratus](./pisistratus.md) - Son of Nestor and companion to Telemachus.
* [Polites](./polites.md) - Trusted companion of Odysseus
* [Polybus](./polybus.md) - Suitor killed by Eumaeus, and two unrelated namesakes in Butler's text
* [Polyphemus](./polyphemus.md) - Cyclops, son of Poseidon, and eater of Odysseus's companions.
* [Pontonous](./pontonous.md) - Herald in Alcinous's court
* [Poseidon](./poseidon.md) - God of the sea and divine antagonist of Odysseus.
* [Proteus](./proteus.md) - Shape-shifting Old Man of the Sea.
* [Scylla](./scylla.md) - Six-headed cliff monster opposite Charybdis.
* [Sisyphus](./sisyphus.md) - Tormented king
* [Tantalus](./tantalus.md) - Tormented shade
* [Telemachus](./telemachus.md) - Son of Odysseus and Penelope; the young prince at the center of the Telemachy.
* [The Laestrygonians](./laestrygonians.md) - Cannibal giants who destroy most of Odysseus's fleet.
* [The Lotus-Eaters](./lotus-eaters.md) - Community whose food erases the desire for return.
* [The Sirens](./sirens.md) - Dangerous singers whose knowledge arrests sailors forever.
* [Theoclymenus](./theoclymenus.md) - Fugitive seer whom Telemachus brings to Ithaca.
* [Tiresias](./tiresias.md) - Dead Theban prophet consulted in the underworld.
* [Tyro](./tyro.md) - First heroine in Odysseus's catalogue of women
* [Zeus](./zeus.md) - Chief Olympian and arbiter of the poem's divine politics.
