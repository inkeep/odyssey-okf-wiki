## Role in the poem

He hosts Telemachus, narrates his Egyptian wanderings, and reports Proteus's news that Odysseus lives.[^butler]

## Interpretive profile

His delayed return mirrors Odysseus's on a safer scale and shows wealth cannot erase wartime grief.[^butler]
