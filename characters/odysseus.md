## Role in the poem

Books 5–12 recount his wanderings; Books 13–24 test whether the returning stranger can become husband, father, king, and son again.[^butler]

## Interpretive profile

His many-minded adaptability is both saving intelligence and a source of danger: the Cyclops episode turns victory into prolonged exile.[^butler]
