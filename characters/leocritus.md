## Role in the poem

Suitor who dismisses the assembly and threatens resistance. He rejects Mentor's warning and helps dissolve the civic gathering without reform.[^butler]

## Where to read

Books 2 and 22 — he speaks against Telemachus in the assembly and is killed by him in the hall, where Butler writes "Telemachus hit Leocritus son of Evenor in the belly". Butler spells the name Leiocritus in Book 2 and Leocritus in Book 22. This figure may be brief, but the reference enlarges the epic's network of divine, household, heroic, and underworld traditions.
