## Role in the poem

Maternal grandmother of Odysseus. Butler names her once, in Book 19: when the young Odysseus arrives at Parnassus she "threw her arms about him, and kissed his head, and both his beautiful eyes". She does not appear again — the boar hunt, the wound, and the binding of it belong to the sons of Autolycus.[^butler]
