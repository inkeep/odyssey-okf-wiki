> Son of Odysseus and Penelope; the young prince at the center of the Telemachy.

## Role in the poem

In Books 1–4 he calls an assembly and visits Pylos and Sparta; later he joins the return and reckoning.[^butler]

## Interpretive profile

His growth is political and rhetorical, but the reunion also tests how a son enters a violent paternal legacy.[^butler]
