## Role in the poem

He gives Odysseus a sealed bag of adverse winds; the crew opens it near Ithaca, and Aeolus refuses a second rescue.[^butler]

## Interpretive profile

The gift becomes a test of trust, leadership, and the destructive effects of unequal knowledge.[^butler]
