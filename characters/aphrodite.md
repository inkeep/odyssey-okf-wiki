## Role in the poem

Goddess whose affair with Ares is sung by Demodocus. Her comic entrapment with Ares in Hephaestus's net forms a divine inset tale about desire, exposure, and compensation.[^butler]

## Where to read

Books 4, 8, 17, 18, 19 and 20. Butler calls her Venus throughout.
