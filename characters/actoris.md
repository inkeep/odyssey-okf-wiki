## Role in the poem

The servant [Penelope](./penelope.md) brought with her from her father's house, and one of the very few people who knew the secret of the marriage bed — which is why Penelope can name her while testing [Odysseus](./odysseus.md).[^butler]

Butler's translation never uses the name. Where the Greek has *Actoris*, he writes only "the daughter of Actor", so she is absent from the counted figure list on the [Book 23 page](../story/book-23.md) even though she appears in the episode. The name in this guide's title comes from the Greek text, not from Butler.[^butler]
