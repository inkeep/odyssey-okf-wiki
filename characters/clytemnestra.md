> Wife who, with Aegisthus, brings about Agamemnon's death — Butler's Agamemnon says the two were "the death of me between them".

## Role in the poem

Her story functions as the darkest counterexample to Penelope and to safe homecoming.[^butler]

## Interpretive profile

The comparison is rhetorically powerful but also reveals the poem's tendency to generalize women's conduct through opposing exempla.[^butler]
