## Role in the poem

Dawn goddess whose recurring arrival structures epic time. Her repeated rising marks departures, assemblies, labor, and renewed action.[^butler]

## Where to read

Books 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19 and 23 — thirteen of the twenty-four. Butler writes the name as Dawn, and once as Aurora. This figure may be brief, but the reference enlarges the epic's network of divine, household, heroic, and underworld traditions.
