## Role in the poem

Son of Nestor remembered among the honoured dead. His death at Troy deepens Nestor's grief, and in Book 24 the shade of [Agamemnon](./agamemnon.md), speaking to [Achilles](./achilles.md), recalls that Antilochus "had been closer to you than any other of your comrades" once Patroclus was gone.[^butler]
