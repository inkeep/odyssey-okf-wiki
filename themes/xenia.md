## Where it appears

Nestor, Menelaus, Eumaeus, and the Phaeacians practice it; Polyphemus and the suitors violate it in opposite directions.[^butler]

## Interpretive tension

Hospitality can protect the vulnerable, but it also expresses hierarchy and creates obligations that cross generations.[^butler]
