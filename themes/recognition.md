> Identity in the poem is not self-declared once; it is proved through scars, memories, objects, skill, emotion, and secret knowledge.

## Where it appears

Argos recognizes silently, Eurycleia through the scar, Telemachus after transformation, Penelope through the bed, Laertes through orchard memories.[^butler]

## Interpretive tension

Each recognition matches a relationship. No universal credential can replace shared history.[^butler]
