## Where it appears

Athena alters appearances; Odysseus's beggar identity tests servants, suitors, wife, and finally his father; false tales regulate disclosure. Telemachus is the exception — Odysseus reveals himself to his son almost at once, without a test.[^butler]

## Interpretive tension

The strategy protects the vulnerable returner, yet it also delays intimacy and subjects loved ones to calculated uncertainty.[^butler]
