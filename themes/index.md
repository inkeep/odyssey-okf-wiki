# Index

## Theme

* [Disguise and testing](./disguise.md) - Disguise creates an experimental space in which character is revealed before status dictates behavior.
* [Food, appetite, and restraint](./food.md) - Meals can enact civilization, worship, hospitality, temptation, or predatory consumption.
* [Metis: cunning intelligence](./metis.md) - Odyssean intelligence works through timing, indirection, disguise, naming, and the reading of other minds.
* [Mortality, fame, and grief](./mortality.md) - The poem values survival and home against immortal stasis and heroic death, without denying the power of fame.
* [Nostos: homecoming](./nostos.md) - Return is not travel alone but recovery of name, household, kinship, authority, and a livable relation to memory.
* [Recognition and identity](./recognition.md) - Identity in the poem is not self-declared once; it is proved through scars, memories, objects, skill, emotion, and secret knowledge.
* [Storytelling and song](./storytelling.md) - Characters survive and compete by narrating the past; bards transform pain into communal knowledge.
* [Violence, justice, and collective guilt](./violence-and-justice.md) - The suitors' destruction is framed as punishment for predation, conspiracy, and repeated warnings ignored.
* [Women, agency, and power](./women-and-power.md) - Women control crucial thresholds of homecoming as goddesses, queens, hosts, enslaved workers, and figures in cautionary tales.
* [Xenia: hospitality](./xenia.md) - Guest-friendship regulates exchange among strangers through food, shelter, gifts, questions, and safe conduct.
